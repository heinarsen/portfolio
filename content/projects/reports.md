### February 2020

## Problem

TradeDesk is an inventory management platform for ticket brokers to buy, sell, and control their tickets. Not only does TradeDesk serve as a broker’s main management system and point of sale, but it is also a platform to analyze their current sales, identify trends, and use data visualization to truly understand their business.

## Solution

Ticket Brokers need an improved, dynamic reporting tool they can use to schedule, organize, and store information regarding their sales, since the current reporting tool does not allow much flexibility and they rely on this feature to maintain and track their sales.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/61f087014bd85c3adea3643a/61f420abf1c4ef7a349e6ce4_Mask%20Group%20(3).jpg)

### Suppliers need a way to schedule their reporting, and enhance their report details page.

**Incorporate Report Scheduling Functionality**

Our users specifically requested a feature that would allow them to schedule their reports to run automatically. Just like many scheduling systems, (whether that be scheduling an email or a recurring meeting), the user can decide on which day and how often they would like to run the report.

After inputting their report details, the user can choose whether or not they would like to schedule the report. From here, they can choose the frequency, on which day and time they would like it to run, and if they want to send the report to any colleagues.

**Enhance Report Details Page**

There are over 36 data points that are pulled from a single report. In order to make this amount of data clear and easily digestible, my team and I grouped relevant information in separate containers. For the actual report details, we used a zebra grid to break up the information and make it easier to consume. The user also has the option of turning off the visibility of certain columns if the information does not apply to them.
