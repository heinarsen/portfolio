### January 2020

## Problem

Ticket brokers need a centralized place they can refer to in order to fulfill the most urgent tasks when it comes to their business, since many requests are time sensitive and must be brought to their attention as soon as possible.

## Solution

Determine The Most Important Tasks Provide Contextual Information Allow Quick Access

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/61f087014bd85c3adea3643a/61f4196c8b75b1992377beed_Mask%20Group%20(1).jpg)

### Process

**1. Determine The Most Important Tasks**

For a larger brokerages, each employee holds a specific role. One broker may be in charge of fulfilling orders, whereas another may be in charge of determining the most competitive pricing for their tickets. Since each employee has a different task, they need a customizable dashboard that is suited to their role. We spoke to our users, and they determined that across the business, the four most important notifications they would want to know when logging into TradeDesk are:

1. Unfulfilled Orders
2. Ticket Listing Errors
3. Expiring Events
4. Comparable Pricing To Other Ticket Groups

My team and I took these four data points and created a dashboard that would highlight and notify our users as soon as they log in.

**2. Provide Contextual Information**

Rather than just stating which ticket groups need attention, we decided to highlight exactly what it is they need to do on the banner below the ticket group information.

![](/images/d3.jpg)

**3. Allow Quick Access**

When the user determines which action item needs more attention, they can go directly to the ticket group by selecting 'view’.
