### March 2020

## Problem

Ticket brokers need a visual tool to help them understand the market of a particular event through color indicators and quickly editable content, since pricing is very competitive, constantly changing, and knowing how to accurately price ticket groups is particularly difficult without understanding the venue's structure and framework.

## Solution

In February 2020, my team and I introduced an interactive seat map integration to TradeDesk. This would be a major enhancement to our tool, giving our users a visual aid into individual venues to understand seat to stage proximity, pricing trends for nearby ticket groups, and the layout of a given venue. We would enable users to view ticket status by color, and make the most important actions available directly on the map.

**2. Identify Most Important Actions & Make Them Accessible**

Before deciding which actions the user will be able to take from the map, we (OF COURSE!) had to talk to our users. They told us that having the ability to change the **status** and the **price** of their ticket groups are two predominant actions they take. With that, we tested out this ability from a map view.
