Spotify Wrapped

November 2021

The Idea

‍I make connections with others through sharing music, talking about music, or playing music. I thought of an idea that could bring similar music fans together by rewarding Spotify users for their dedication. Building off of 'Spotify Wrapped', a campaign that compiles a users' listening data and displays their most listened to songs, artists, and genres, Wrapped Sessions is a curated listening experience that gives eligibility for listeners to access remarkable experiences. For users with over 100 hours of listening to a particular artist, they gain eligibility to that artist's live performances, DJ sets, and similar events during the Wrapped season.

The Execution

A Spotify feature that compiles user data, then lets them know which events they're eligible for during the Wrapped season. Given that the user has gained eligibility, they can purchase tickets through Songkick for any appropriate show across the US. They can also bring up to 2 guests. Users gain entry to exclusive events, they can meet other fans, and make lifelong connections with other music lovers.‍Full case study coming soon.
